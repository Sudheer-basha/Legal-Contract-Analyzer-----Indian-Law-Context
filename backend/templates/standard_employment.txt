Standard Employment Agreement Template - Indian Law defaults

1. Duties and Services: The Employee agrees to perform the duties of the assigned role diligently and in accordance with company policies.

2. Non-Compete Restriction: The Employee agrees that during their employment and for a period of 1 (one) year post termination, they shall not engage in any competing business. (Note: Under Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act 1872, post-employment non-competes are generally void).

3. Intellectual Property Ownership: Any intellectual property or inventions created by the Employee during the hours of employment and utilizing company resources shall vest exclusively in the Company.

4. Termination Notice: Either party may terminate this agreement by providing 30 (thirty) days written notice, or payment of salary in lieu of notice.

5. Dispute Resolution: Any disputes arising out of this employment contract shall be resolved by arbitration in Bangalore in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
